Building a path to real-time gauge dynamics.

SU2QC is a multi-institution research collaboration developing AI-accelerated methods to study non-Abelian lattice gauge theories on quantum hardware.

Explore the research View research library

Physics-first. Hardware-aware.

We connect field-theory structure to practical execution, preserving physical constraints while reducing the cost of simulation.

Gauge dynamics

Real-time non-Abelian dynamics, from confinement and flux tubes to string breaking.

Quantum hardware

Symmetry-preserving encodings and hardware-aware circuits for emerging quantum systems.

AI acceleration

Learning-assisted compilation, circuit optimization, measurement, and error mitigation.

Hybrid computing

Quantum, GPU, tensor-network, and high-performance workflows designed to work together.

One team across physics and computing.
