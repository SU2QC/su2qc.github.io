Principal Investigator

Raza Sabbir Sufian

Assistant Professor of Physics
New Mexico State University

A theoretical and computational nuclear and particle physicist whose research connects lattice QCD, hadron structure, phenomenology, and quantum simulation. He earned his Ph.D. at the University of Kentucky and held research appointments at Jefferson Lab and the RIKEN-BNL Research Center before joining NMSU.
